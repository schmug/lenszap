Inside the district's new incident response plan

The district's plan names a single person to call when systems go down, and lists which vendors must be told within a day. It was written after last spring's outage and reviewed by the county.

A page from the plan, as posted on the district's website. Image: Riverside Ledger.

Teachers will get a printed checklist for taking attendance on paper, and the plan sets out how grades entered offline are reconciled later. Board members asked for a drill before spring testing.

The superintendent summed up the plan at Tuesday's meeting:

Every school will know who to call, what to switch off, and how to keep teaching while the systems are down.
